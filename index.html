Tradition

How an Athangudi tile is made

Glass base

The tile is cast face down on a glass plate, which gives the surface its smooth, glossy finish.

Colour layer

Cement mixed with oxide pigments is poured inside a mould frame to draw the pattern.

Dry mix

Dry cement and fine sand are dusted on top to take up the moisture.

Backing layer

A sand and cement layer goes over it. This gives the tile its body and strength.

Cure and dry

The tile is lifted, cured in water and dried before it is ready for your floor.
